COMMAND CARDS

  • Blessing of the Gods: Play any time during your activation on one of your models. That model's weapons gain Blessed and Damage Type: Magical this activation.
  • Defenses: Play at the start of the game after turn order, terrain, and objectives are placed, but before deploying. First player: up to two Defenses. Second player: up to three Defenses. If both play, take turns placing, starting with second player. Place within 24" of rear edge of Deployment Zone. Cannot be placed within 3" of an objective, obstruction, obstacle, or another Defense.
  • Duck and Cover!: Play any time during activation of one of your warrior units. Models gain cover and Resistance: Blast for one round.
  • Lucky Penny: Play at the start of a friendly model's activation. Give model one corpse, essence, focus, fury, rage, or soul token.
  • Old Faithful: Play during Control Phase before allocating focus. Remove d3+1 damage from a friendly Cohort model or battle engine.

Defenses Details

General: A Defense is removed if contacted by a model with an extra-large or huge base.

  • Barrier

    Obstacle: This terrain feature is an obstacle.

    Barrier Rule: When a large- or smaller-based model without Flight or Pathfinder attempts to cross, reduce movement by 2".

  • Fire Pit

    Obstacle: This terrain feature is an obstacle.

    Cover: This terrain feature grants cover.

    Fire Hazard: Hazard. Model without Flight entering suffers Fire continuous effect. If a trooper model without Flight advances through as part of Normal Movement, all models in unit suffer fire continuous effect.

    Illumination: Models lose Stealth while within 5".

  • Spike Trap

    Spike Hazard: Hazard. Enemy model without Flight entering or ending activation within 1" suffers POW 10 damage. Enemy warjack without Flight entering also suffers 1 damage to first available Movement System box. Your models don't suffer if you placed them.

    Rough Terrain: This terrain feature is rough terrain.
